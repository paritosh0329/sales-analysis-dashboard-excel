# Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data and presents key business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Cleaning
- Dashboard Design

## Dashboard Features
- Total Orders
- Total Revenue
- Average Customer Spend
- Average Order Delivery Time
- Revenue by Occasion
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Hour
- Revenue by Month
- Top 5 Products
- Top 10 Cities

# Executive Summary – Ferns & Petals Sales Analysis

This dashboard analyzes 1,000 customer orders placed across multiple occasions, product categories, cities, and time periods. The objective of the analysis was to identify revenue drivers, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and operational efficiency.

## Key Business Metrics

* Total Orders Processed: 1,000
* Total Revenue Generated: ₹35,20,984
* Average Customer Spend: ₹3,520.98 per order
* Average Order-to-Delivery Time: 5.53 days

## Revenue Analysis

Occasion-based analysis shows that Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generated the highest revenue among all occasions, while Diwali and Valentine's Day contributed comparatively lower revenue. This indicates stronger customer demand during family-oriented celebrations and gifting occasions.

Category-wise revenue analysis reveals that Colors generated the highest revenue, followed by Soft Toys and Sweets. These categories represent the strongest contributors to overall sales and should be prioritized for inventory planning and promotional campaigns.

## Customer Purchase Behavior

The revenue-by-hour analysis indicates that customer purchases are concentrated during specific hours of the day, suggesting clear peak ordering periods. Understanding these patterns can help optimize marketing campaigns and staffing requirements during high-demand hours.

Monthly revenue trends show significant fluctuations throughout the year, with noticeable peaks in February, March, and August. These periods align closely with major gifting occasions and seasonal demand.

## Product Performance

Among the top-performing products, Magnum Set, Quia Gift, Harum Pack, Dolores Gift, and Deserunt Box generated the highest revenues. These products consistently contributed to sales performance and can be considered high-value offerings.

## Geographic Insights

City-level analysis indicates that cities such as Imphal, Dhanbad, Kavali, and Haridwar recorded the highest order volumes. These locations represent strong customer markets and may provide opportunities for targeted marketing and expansion initiatives.

## Operational Performance

The average delivery time of 5.53 days demonstrates a relatively stable fulfillment process. Continued monitoring of delivery timelines can help improve customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## Recommendations

* Increase promotional activities around Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan occasions.
* Focus inventory management on high-performing categories such as Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets.
* Expand marketing efforts in high-order cities to maximize revenue potential.
* Analyze peak ordering hours for targeted advertising campaigns.
* Reduce delivery time further to enhance customer experience and repeat purchases.

This dashboard provides actionable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, and operational efficiency, enabling data-driven decision-making for business growth.
